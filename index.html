I am a PhD candidate in Creative Informatics at The University of Tokyo. My research focuses on human–machine interaction (HMI) for autonomous vehicles — designing pedestrian–AV interaction approaches validated through VR simulation and real-world field studies.

In parallel, I contribute to the Autoware open-source stack at Tier IV, improving CI reliability and running physical robot experiments.
