Email Scholar GitHub Website

I am a PhD candidate in Creative Informatics at The University of Tokyo, advised in the Tsukada-Fujiwara Lab. My research focuses on human–machine interaction (HMI) for autonomous vehicles — I design and prototype pedestrian–AV interaction approaches (eHMIs, smart infrastructure) and validate them through VR simulation and real-world field studies.

In parallel, I contribute to the Autoware open-source autonomous driving stack at Tier IV, improving scenario-test CI reliability and running physical robot experiments.
